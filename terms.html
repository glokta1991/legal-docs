3. Protocol Guard & Discovery Policy (v54.0.0)Protocol Guard & Entdeckungsrichtlinie (v54.0.0)

Messages are End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE). The developer has zero visibility into message and attachment content and contacts; the server acts only as a relay for encrypted blobs. Metadata is minimized, not eliminated. StateZer0 stores the identity key in the Android Keystore as a device-bound key; it is non-exportable by normal means. On the server, MongoDB fields are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using GHOST_FIELD_KEY; message and attachment payloads are opaque encrypted blobs to the relay. Accounts are hidden from search by default. Adding contacts requires mutual-consent invite requests with Accept/Reject; the relay does not maintain a social graph. Delivered messages and media are erased from the relay within 24 hours (or 24 hours after acknowledgment); undelivered content is erased within 72 hours. Nachrichten sind Ende-zu-Ende-verschlüsselt (E2EE). Der Entwickler hat keinerlei Einblick in Inhalte von Nachrichten und Anhängen sowie in Kontakte; der Server dient ausschließlich als Relay für verschlüsselte Blobs. Metadaten werden minimiert, nicht eliminiert. StateZer0 speichert den Identitätsschlüssel im Android-Keystore als gerätegebundenen Schlüssel; er ist unter normalen Umständen nicht exportierbar. Konten sind standardmäßig vor der Suche verborgen.
